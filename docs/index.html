Data: Hansen/UMD/Google/USGS/NASA Global Forest Change v1.12 (2000 to 2024). Forest means 30% or more tree canopy in 2000. Boundaries: geoBoundaries gbOpen (OpenStreetMap-derived, CC BY-SA). Limits: tree cover loss includes harvested plantations, shade-tree thinning and fire, so a high value means "look closer", not "deforestation". Values do not include gain, and cocoa or oil palm areas are not separated out. Areas are approximate.
